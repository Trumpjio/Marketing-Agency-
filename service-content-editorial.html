Getting started

The first 30 days.

Three things happen when we start: we identify the tests, gather the data, and set expectations against a realistic timeline.

What we test
  • Pillar topic selection — the 3–5 topics you can realistically own.
  • Author voice test — ghostwriting a founder piece vs their own voice.
  • Format test — long-form essay vs structured guide vs case study.
  • Newsletter cadence — weekly vs biweekly vs monthly.
  • Distribution channel — which platform amplifies the piece best.
What we need from you
  • Current content inventory (CSV of posts, performance).
  • Access to Google Search Console and GA4.
  • Newsletter list and ESP access (Mailchimp, Beehiiv, Substack, etc.).
  • Interview availability — founder, SMEs, customers.
  • CRM data on which content types correlate to deals.

What goes wrong

The common mistakes.

The patterns we see repeated across underperforming programs. Fix any of these and performance usually moves immediately.

01

Using AI to write finished copy.

Google down-ranks it. Buyers notice. Your brand voice erodes.

02

Publishing without a plan to promote.

Great writing with no distribution is a journal, not a strategy.

03

Writing for 'everyone' and reaching no one.

Specificity wins. Niche audiences compound faster than broad ones.

04

Letting content and SEO work in silos.

Content without entities + schema is content that doesn't rank.

Questions, answered

Common questions.

The real questions prospects ask us before signing an engagement.

Do you use AI to write?+

For research summaries and first-draft scaffolding, yes. For published prose, no. AI content ranks poorly, degrades brand voice and is increasingly down-ranked by Google.

How many pieces per month?+

Quality over cadence. Typically 2–6 long-form pieces monthly, plus a weekly newsletter and supporting shorter content.

Can you ghostwrite for founders?+

Yes — one of our most-requested services. We interview, draft, and iterate until the voice is unmistakably theirs.

How long is a 'long-form' piece?+

Usually 1,500–4,000 words, depending on topic and intent. Shorter when the question is specific. Longer when the piece is a definitive resource.

What if I don't want to be the 'author'?+

We can set up house editorial under a brand name, or named-author programs for several team members, or a mix. We'll recommend what fits your buyer.

How do you handle Arabic content?+

Native Arabic writers for both MSA and dialect. Research and editorial standards are the same across languages.

Can content really outperform ads?+

Over a 2–3 year horizon, yes. One strong pillar essay can drive more qualified leads over 24 months than most 30-day paid campaigns.

Ready to grow?

We take on a limited number of partnerships each quarter. Thirty minutes, no pitch — a frank conversation about where your growth is leaking and what it would take to seal it.
