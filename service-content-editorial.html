FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you use AI to write?+
For research summaries and first-draft scaffolding, yes. For published prose, no. AI content ranks poorly, degrades brand voice, and is increasingly down-ranked by Google.
How many pieces per month?+
Quality over cadence. Typically 2–6 long-form pieces a month, plus a weekly newsletter and supporting shorter content.
Can you ghostwrite for founders?+
Yes — one of our most-requested services. We interview, draft, and iterate until the voice is unmistakably theirs.
How is content tied to SEO?+
Briefs are built around entities and intents from the SEO strategy, not keyword stuffing. Every piece has a target position in the funnel and a target ranking, so results are measurable.
