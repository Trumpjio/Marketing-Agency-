Muharraq.
Aviation, logistics, and the growing commercial second city.

Muharraq, home to Bahrain International Airport and the country's logistics backbone, has distinct dynamics from Manama proper. It was Bahrain's capital until 1923, and it's now scaling fast on the back of aviation, logistics, construction and emerging commercial real estate like Diyar Al Muharraq.
