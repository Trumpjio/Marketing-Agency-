Qatar
— Compact market. Outsized ambition.

Qatar has among the highest GDP per capita on Earth, a post-FIFA-2022 tourism legacy, and a Qatar National Vision 2030 agenda investing in real estate, hospitality, retail and a diversified non-energy economy. Doha is positioning itself (via Web Summit Qatar, among others) as the Middle East's digital capital.

Why the generic playbook fails.

These are the market-specific dynamics that most agencies miss when they port a template from a different country.

01 —

Buyers start in AI assistants.

Research by Qatar-based agencies notes buyers in Doha are 'increasingly likely to start research inside an AI assistant rather than a search bar'. AEO/GEO is foundational, not optional.

02 —

Small market, high stakes.

Reputational damage spreads fast in a compact market. Discretion is a service, not a nice-to-have.

03 —

Short-form video dominates attention.

Reels, TikToks and Shorts take the lion's share of digital time.

04 —

Hyper-local targeting works.

District-level targeting (The Pearl vs West Bay vs Lusail) is a real segmentation signal in Doha.
