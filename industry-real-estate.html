Channels

Where to invest. Where not to.

Not every channel earns a place in a Real Estate plan. Here's where the budget belongs and where it burns.

Where to invest

  • Property platforms (Property Finder, Bayut, LuxuryProperty)
    Still the primary discovery for serious property buyers in UAE/KSA/Qatar.
  • Google Search + AI Overviews
    High-intent researchers. AEO infrastructure essential.
  • Instagram and YouTube Shorts
    Virtual tours, drone footage, lifestyle content, neighbourhood explainers.
  • Meta paid lead ads
    Form-fill lead generation at scale. Works especially well in Dubai and Riyadh.
  • Snapchat
    Especially in Saudi Arabia younger investor audience.
  • Email + WhatsApp follow-up
    Where multi-week sales cycles get closed.

Where not to

  • Pinterest, Reddit, Quora for lead generation
    Buyers aren't researching your inventory here.
  • Generic banner / programmatic without creative
    Burns budget. Property buyers ignore it.
  • TV / print only, no digital follow-up
    A dead medium for property discovery. Only works with digital reinforcement.
  • Vanity campaigns without lead capture
    Beautiful awareness films that drive zero inquiries.
The campaign shape

What actually converts.

Offer, landing page and messaging the three things most Real Estate campaigns get wrong.

The offer

Property marketing offers work best when they're specific. 'Luxury 2BR apartments from AED X at Palm Jumeirah book a viewing' outperforms 'luxury living in Dubai'. Finance structures (0% down, post-handover plans, flexible payment) are massive conversion drivers in UAE and KSA.

The landing page

Dedicated per-development landing pages with: hero drone/CGI, floor plans, pricing, finance options, virtual tour, booking form with WhatsApp option. Not the homepage. Not the brand site.

The follow-up

Property sales close over 3–8 weeks on average. A disciplined cadence of WhatsApp follow-ups + personalised emails + optional calls, timed to buyer momentum signals, beats aggressive daily spam every time.

The operating system

How we run a Real Estate program.

Three parts of the system: what we test first, what data we need, and what we measure.

What we test
  • Dedicated landing page per project vs generic brand site.
  • WhatsApp-first lead capture vs form-fill.
  • Finance-led creative vs lifestyle-led creative.
  • Property-platform listing optimisation (title, photos, description).
  • Walk-in appointment booking vs 'enquire now' different conversion rates entirely.
What we need
  • Full inventory list with pricing and availability.
  • Historical lead data by source + conversion to sale.
  • Sales team's qualification criteria and process.
  • CRM access (Salesforce, HubSpot, local CRM).
  • Property platform listings and performance data.
What we measure
  • Qualified leads per channel (not raw lead count).
  • Walk-in to sale conversion rate.
  • Cost per qualified lead by project and channel.
  • Sales cycle length by lead source.
  • Revenue per marketing dollar by project.

Questions, answered

Common questions.

The real questions prospects ask us before signing an engagement.

What's the realistic monthly marketing budget for a property project?+

In UAE/KSA, $20–60K per month per active project is typical for serious developers. Brokers can start lower if they focus on specific projects or neighbourhoods.

Should we run paid media or build organic?+

Both. Paid media captures the active buyers now; organic (SEO, content, social) compounds over 12+ months. Developers who skip organic pay more every year to generate the same leads.

Why isn't my Property Finder listing performing?+

Usually: weak photography, missing floor plans, no price, generic description, poor response time. Platform-listing optimisation is a specific craft most agencies ignore.

How important is WhatsApp for property leads?+

In GCC, WhatsApp is often the primary conversion channel. Buyers who message on WhatsApp close 2–3× faster than form-fill leads.

Can one agency handle a developer and a broker in the same market?+

We generally don't conflict of interest. We pick the engagement that fits our vertical specialism better.

Should I pay for a vanity brand film?+

Only if it's part of a full-funnel plan. A beautiful film without lead-capture, retargeting and WhatsApp follow-up is money burned.
