Questions, answered

Common questions.

From engagements and from the questions buyers actually ask us not generic SEO filler.

Why is Snapchat so much more important in Qatar than other GCC markets?+

A combination of cultural comfort with the platform's AR features, high affluence, and strong local creator economy. Qatar sees Snap usage patterns that most Western markets wouldn't recognise campaign-grade, not casual.

Why focus on AI search for Doha specifically?+

Doha buyers skew early-adopter, highly educated, English-fluent and research-heavy. The result: Perplexity, ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews are everyday research tools for a bigger share of Doha's affluent segment than almost anywhere globally.

Pearl vs Lusail vs West Bay how do I target each?+

Pearl: ultra-luxury, lifestyle, hospitality. Lusail: premium residential, younger professional, newer brands. West Bay: corporate, finance, government-adjacent. Each gets its own creative, media mix, and often its own landing page.

Is the FIFA 2022 tourism legacy still relevant for marketing?+

Yes. Infrastructure, hotel capacity and global brand recognition built around FIFA continue to drive sustained tourism. Operators who tap it (hotels, airlines, DMCs) still see outsized returns.

Can an agency outside Qatar work with Qatari clients?+

Yes, but local presence and face-to-face relationships still matter. Pure-remote agencies win work less often than agencies with a Doha presence.
