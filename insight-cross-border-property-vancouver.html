Looking to market cross-border property investment in Vancouver?

Vancouver's luxury property market runs on international buyer flows. Here is how to market cross-border property investment credibly, including to the growing Middle Eastern segment.

Book a strategy call
About marketing in Vancouver · All insights

Vancouver property marketing is a tri-language exercise.

No other North American property market is as internationally exposed as Vancouver. Mandarin, Arabic and English are the three native-language layers most luxury property programmes need to run simultaneously. Running only in English leaves large and specifically-wealthy buyer segments to competitors.

The Middle Eastern buyer segment is growing and underserved.

Mandarin-speaking Chinese buyers have dominated Vancouver's international-buyer narrative for a decade. In the last few years, a growing segment of Middle Eastern and Gulf buyers has entered the market — and most agencies have not caught up. Arabic creative, Gulf-oriented media buying and Dubai-based intermediary networks are routinely absent.

What this buyer looks for.

Climate, education for children, security, long-term capital preservation and lifestyle. Not yield. The messaging that converts emphasises family, legacy, lifestyle quality and school access — not investment-return charts.

The marketing playbook.

YouTube and Instagram-led. Property films that tell the lifestyle story, not just the building story. Arabic creative for the private-sales funnel. Trusted Gulf intermediaries who speak to the Vancouver programme regularly. WhatsApp as the primary private-communication channel once interest is live.

Where campaigns fail.

Translated English brochures. Generic 'international luxury' creative. Lack of named Arabic-speaking relationship managers on the Vancouver side. Over-reliance on digital with no in-market intermediary trust layer. These are fixable but systemic failures across the Vancouver luxury property programme as it exists today.
