Who they serve

The customers behind the market.

Jeddah's buyers skew more relaxed, more culturally confident, and more cosmopolitan than Riyadh. Hejazi families with multi-generational wealth. Religious tourists year-round, with huge spikes around hajj and umrah. Red Sea tourism visitors. Expatriate communities with deep roots.

The Jeddah playbook

How marketing differs here.

The things most agencies miss when they port a playbook in from somewhere else.

01 —

Hejazi dialect matters.

Content shot and written for Jeddah needs to feel Jeddah — not generic MSA.

02 —

Plan around the religious calendar.

Hajj, umrah and Ramadan are the biggest marketing moments of the year. Build the calendar around them.

03 —

WhatsApp is a channel, not just a tool.

Jeddah buyers expect to be able to WhatsApp a business and get a quick answer. Build for it.

04 —

Snapchat creator partnerships.

Local Snap creators have outsized influence in Jeddah consumer categories. Make it a program, not a one-off.
