The Jeddah marketing calendar

Religious tourism moves the market. Build around it.

Jeddah's biggest commercial moments map to the Islamic calendar not the Gregorian one. These are the four periods where getting the campaign right matters most.

Ramadan
The biggest retail, F&B and content moment of the year. Begin planning six months out.
Hajj
Religious tourism peak. Hotels, airport services, hospitality, F&B. Operators who prepare early win.
Umrah peak months
Smaller than Hajj but longer. A sustained tourism demand window spring and autumn.
Red Sea tourism season
Separate from religious tourism. Autumn and winter are the premium months.
Saudi National Day
September a major brand and campaign moment across all categories.
Shopping festivals & Jeddah Season
Government-backed campaigns driving retail and hospitality traffic.

Questions, answered

Common questions.

From engagements and from the questions buyers actually ask us not generic SEO filler.

How different is Jeddah from Riyadh for marketing?+

Different dialect, different consumer rhythm, different priority industries. Jeddah leans more cosmopolitan and family-retail. Riyadh leans more enterprise and entertainment. Dialect, calendar and platform mix all shift.

How should I plan around Hajj and Umrah?+

They dominate the year. Hajj and the Umrah peak months drive religious tourism marketing demand. Ramadan drives retail, F&B and content consumption. Build the editorial and media calendar backwards from these moments.

Hejazi dialect do I really need it?+

For Jeddah consumer categories, yes. Hejazi feels native and local. MSA Arabic feels corporate. For B2B English-first with Hejazi social is often the right mix.

Which Jeddah neighbourhoods matter most?+

Al Hamra, Al Shati and the Corniche for luxury and premium. Al Rawdah and Al Zahra for mid-market. Al Balad for heritage tourism. Jeddah Islamic Port district for B2B logistics.

Can I run Jeddah campaigns from Riyadh?+

Possible but suboptimal. Native Hejazi creative, local creator partnerships and in-city presence outperform remote operations. Many of our Jeddah engagements have been strongest when run with a Jeddah-based lead.
