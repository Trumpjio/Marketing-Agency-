Questions, answered

Common questions.

From engagements and from the questions buyers actually ask us — not generic SEO filler.

How much does digital marketing actually cost in Dubai?+

For paid media alone, you need around $25K per month to generate useful learning data. Below that, Dubai CPMs eat the budget before the campaign gets smarter. SEO and content programmes can start smaller and compound.

Which neighbourhoods should I target for real estate?+

Depends on your product. Palm Jumeirah and Downtown for ultra-luxury. Dubai Marina, JBR and Business Bay for mid-market premium. Dubai Hills and MBR City for family premium. Target each separately — don't bundle.

Is Snapchat still worth running in Dubai?+

For local Emirati audiences, especially under 30, yes. For expat-heavy premium categories, Instagram and YouTube outperform. Use Snapchat with intent, not as a tactical afterthought.

How is Dubai different from Abu Dhabi for marketing?+

Dubai weighs more toward retail, hospitality, D2C and media. Abu Dhabi weighs more toward government, energy, finance and culture. A single pan-UAE campaign usually underperforms both. We build them as separate plans.

How long before I see SEO results in Dubai?+

Technical and schema fixes land in weeks. Rankings for competitive terms typically move over 3–6 months. Compounding gains past month 9. AEO wins (showing up in AI Overviews) can land faster if your schema and FAQ pages are right.

What's the best platform for a new F&B launch in Dubai?+

Instagram first, with heavy Reels production. TikTok as a strong second for younger segments. Local Google Maps SEO for discovery. Add influencer partnerships from day one. F&B is a visual, social-led category in Dubai.
