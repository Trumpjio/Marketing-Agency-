Market dynamics

What's actually different here.

The things most agencies miss when they treat Dubai as "another Gulf city".

01 —

Among the highest ad costs in the region.

Dubai CPMs across Meta and Google regularly top the GCC. Winning here is a creative problem, not a budget problem.

02 —

Multilingual, multinational buyers.

A single campaign may need to serve English, Arabic, Hindi, Russian and Mandarin audiences — with different cultural cues.

03 —

Platform mix skews premium.

Instagram, YouTube and Google dominate. TikTok is strong but less dominant than in KSA. Snapchat is still relevant for younger local Emirati audiences.

04 —

AI-first search is already here.

Perplexity, ChatGPT Search and Google AI Overviews are everyday research tools for Dubai's affluent buyers. Schema, FAQPage, and llms.txt are table stakes.

Questions buyers ask

Common questions about marketing in Dubai.

Answers from engagements and from buyer research — not generic SEO filler.

What's the minimum budget to do digital marketing in Dubai properly?+
For paid media alone, around $25K/month. Below that, Dubai's CPMs eat the budget before the campaign can learn.
Is Arabic content a must in Dubai?+
English is the commercial lingua franca, but Arabic content materially expands reach among local Emirati and Khaleeji buyers — especially in luxury, hospitality, and family-oriented categories.
Which platforms matter most for a premium brand in Dubai?+
Instagram, YouTube, and Google Search drive most premium decisions. LinkedIn for B2B. TikTok as a top-of-funnel platform. Snapchat for younger local audiences.
Do we need a physical office in Dubai to work with an agency here?+
No, but your agency should have one. We do.
