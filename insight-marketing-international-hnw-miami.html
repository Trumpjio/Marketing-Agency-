Looking to market Miami real estate to international HNW buyers?

The Dubai-Miami property corridor is one of the busiest HNW real estate flows in the world. Here is what marketing to international buyers in Miami actually looks like.

About marketing in Miami · All insights

The Dubai-Miami corridor is real.

Over the last five years, Miami has become one of the top international HNW destinations for Gulf capital. The reasons are specific: climate, no state income tax, cultural internationalism, direct flight connectivity, and a strong Latin American diaspora creating a cosmopolitan feel that Gulf buyers find comfortable.

The buyer universe is not uniform.

GCC HNW buyers look at Miami differently from Latin American HNW buyers, who look at it differently from European or Russian buyers. Lumping them together is why most Miami property marketing feels generic. The GCC segment specifically wants: beachfront or marina views, security, discretion, concierge-level service, and increasingly Shariah-compliant financing structures.

The marketing that converts.

Instagram-first with video priority. Property films, not property stills. Arabic creative where it earns its place (particularly the private-sales funnel). Named relationship managers. WhatsApp as the primary private-communication channel once interest is confirmed.

Why generalist agencies underperform.

Most Miami property marketing is built for domestic buyers plus a generic 'international' overlay. The GCC segment is specific enough — and spends enough — to warrant its own creative, its own media plan and its own CRM workflow. Treating it as an afterthought leaves significant volume on the table.

Where to start.

A dedicated GCC-focused campaign track running alongside your existing programme. Arabic creative for the top-funnel awareness layer. Instagram and YouTube property films localised in tone. A trusted intermediary network in Dubai and Riyadh. Private-channel communication from first qualified interest.
