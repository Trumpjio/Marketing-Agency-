FAQ

Questions, answered.

Native or cross-platform?+
Depends on what the app needs. Heavy on device APIs, animation, or offline — go native. Content-driven, lots of parity between iOS and Android — cross-platform is often faster and cheaper. We will give you a straight recommendation, not a preference.
Can you take over an existing codebase?+
Yes. We start with a code review and risk audit, then stabilize before we add features. Rewrites are a last resort, never a first move.
What about backend and infrastructure?+
We can build and operate the backend (Node, Go or Python, hosted on AWS or GCP), or integrate with your existing team.
How is this priced?+
Fixed-price for a scoped MVP, then a retainer for ongoing development and growth. No open-ended hourly billing.
