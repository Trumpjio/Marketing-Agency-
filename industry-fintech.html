In fintech, buyers trust what they can verify.

Fintech marketing is different. Buyers are regulated. Content has to be accurate. Compliance isn't optional. The brands winning in GCC fintech are the ones that treat marketing as a trust engineering problem — not a performance one.

Channels

Where to invest. Where not to.

Not every channel earns a place in a Fintech plan. Here's where the budget belongs — and where it burns.

Where to invest

  • LinkedIn
    Core B2B channel for fintech buyers in GCC and beyond.
  • Google Search + AI Overviews
    High-intent product research. AEO infrastructure critical.
  • Long-form content and SEO
    The buyer trusts depth. Thin content signals risk.
  • Industry events and sponsorships
    Fintech buyers meet suppliers in person first.
  • Email + account-based marketing (ABM)
    Where enterprise deals get closed.
  • Earned PR in financial publications
    Trust is manufactured over months, not days.

Where not to

  • TikTok / Snapchat for enterprise fintech
    Wrong audience, wrong trust context.
  • Instagram as a primary B2B channel
    Secondary at best for enterprise.
  • Generic programmatic
    Rarely moves fintech buyers who demand transparency and specificity.
  • Clickbait content strategies
    Compounds brand damage in a trust-first category.
The campaign shape

What actually converts.

Offer, landing page and messaging — the three things most Fintech campaigns get wrong.

The offer

Fintech offers work best when they're product-specific and risk-mitigated. 'Settle merchant payments in 24h — free integration review' outperforms 'faster payments'. Pilots, sandboxes and free trials with real support are the strongest conversion mechanisms.

The landing page

Product-specific landing pages with: clear use case, API docs linked, compliance badges (regulator mentions, SOC2, PCI, Shariah where relevant), case studies with named customers, pricing or 'request quote' CTA, demo video, security FAQ.

The follow-up

Enterprise fintech sales cycles are 3–12 months. Sequenced email + LinkedIn outreach + content + webinar invitations across that window. ABM-grade personalisation on named accounts beats mass outreach by 10×.

The operating system

How we run a Fintech program.

Three parts of the system: what we test first, what data we need, and what we measure.

What we test
  • Product-specific landing pages vs single-product site.
  • Technical content vs business-outcome content for primary ad traffic.
  • LinkedIn ABM vs cold outbound vs event-driven acquisition.
  • Webinar vs guide vs assessment as primary MOFU lead magnet.
  • Named customer logos above vs below the fold.
What we need
  • Current CRM and sales process documentation.
  • Named customer case studies and testimonials.
  • Compliance and regulatory landscape documentation.
  • Target account list (ICP) with named prospects.
  • Historical lead-source data by deal size.
What we measure
  • Marketing-qualified leads (MQLs) by account tier.
  • Pipeline sourced by marketing vs sales.
  • Account engagement scores (ABM programs).
  • Content-attributed deals.
  • AI engine citations and rankings on key product queries.

Questions, answered

Common questions.

The real questions prospects ask us before signing an engagement.

Why does AEO/GEO matter so much for fintech?+

Enterprise buyers are increasingly researching in Perplexity and ChatGPT Search. If you're not cited there for your category's key queries, you're invisible in the first stage of their research.

How do we handle regulatory compliance in marketing?+

Every campaign goes through regulatory review before shipping. We work with your compliance team early, not after a shutdown.

How long does a fintech brand program take to build?+

Visible authority in 6–12 months. Deep category ownership in 18–24. Trust in a regulated category compounds slowly but defensibly.

Should we invest in paid or organic first?+

Both, but weight toward organic early. Paid without an authority foundation burns cash. Fintech buyers trust what they verify.

How important is LinkedIn ABM for enterprise fintech?+

Core. For target-account-focused enterprise fintech, ABM typically outperforms cold prospecting 3–5× on pipeline conversion.

Can you help with developer-focused marketing?+

Yes. Developer content, documentation UX, API-portal strategy, open-source community programs — all part of the fintech toolkit.
