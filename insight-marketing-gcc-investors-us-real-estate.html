Looking to market US real estate to GCC investors?

What GCC family offices and HNW buyers actually look for when buying US property — and how to market US real estate credibly to them.

Book a strategy call
About marketing in New York · All insights

The Gulf investor you are selling to is not a general international buyer.

Gulf family offices and HNW individuals buying US real estate behave differently from the generic 'international investor' marketing categorises them as. They buy for different reasons, over different timelines, with different trust requirements. Treating them as a generic foreign-buyer segment is why so much US property marketing underperforms in the Gulf.

What they actually look for.

Yield matters, but security, legacy and residency considerations often matter more. US tax exposure is a serious concern. Family office compliance and discretion are non-negotiable. The marketing that converts emphasises these, not just the pretty building and the capital-appreciation chart.

What marketing format works.

English-first with Arabic available on request. Private video walk-throughs over mass campaigns. Named relationship managers over web forms. Trust signals (legal structure, custody, lender partnerships) are more persuasive than lifestyle imagery.

Where campaigns go wrong.

Generic 'luxury Manhattan' campaigns treat Gulf buyers as price-insensitive status consumers. The reality is more sophisticated: Gulf capital is professional, long-horizon, and increasingly structured. Marketing that reads like it is talking down to the buyer gets filed under 'unserious'.

The channels that work.

LinkedIn for the family-office network layer. Direct outreach through trusted intermediaries. Private-channel WhatsApp communication once interest is live. Arabic-language PR in Gulf publications read by UHNW individuals. Email is still central for institutional communication.
