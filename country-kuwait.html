Kuwait
— Compact, affluent, under-served.

Kuwait is a high-income, high-density market where digital marketing demand concentrates in Kuwait City. The digital marketing software market alone is roughly $140M, with growth driven by near-universal smartphone usage, high internet penetration, and a large base of SMEs, family groups and multinationals.

What's different here

Why the generic playbook fails.

These are the market-specific dynamics that most agencies miss when they port a template from a different country.

01 —

Dominated by Kuwait City.

The vast majority of commercial activity is concentrated here. Local targeting beyond the capital rarely moves the number.

02 —

Family-group dynamics.

Many of the largest buyers are family conglomerates. Decision-making is relationship-led, not RFP-led.

03 —

Instagram-first.

Instagram remains the strongest consumer discovery platform. TikTok is rising but less dominant than in KSA.

04 —

Specialist agencies rare.

Most local agencies are generalist. Vertical specialists have a defensible position.
