Questions, answered

Common questions.

From engagements and from the questions buyers actually ask us not generic SEO filler.

Why is Abu Dhabi more B2B-weighted than Dubai?+

Government spend is larger. Energy and sovereign wealth dominate. The buyer profile skews institutional rather than retail. The same brand showing up in both emirates should look and sound different.

ADGM vs DIFC which should I focus on?+

Depends on your product. ADGM has grown fast on crypto/digital-asset regulation, VC and family office. DIFC is still the deeper market for traditional banking, insurance and brokerage. Many of our clients work both; the plan is different for each.

How different are government and sovereign engagements?+

Procurement is slower, more formal, and more credential-driven. Content and PR carry disproportionate weight. Discretion is a service. Pure-performance marketing playbooks don't translate well.

Does LinkedIn really outperform Instagram in Abu Dhabi B2B?+

Yes, for finance, energy and professional services. LinkedIn gets a bigger share of attention from decision-makers than any other channel. Instagram is a strong second for consumer, culture and hospitality not B2B.

Should I run a joint Dubai + Abu Dhabi campaign?+

You can share brand and creative, but the media plan should treat them as separate markets. Audience, platforms, and messaging mix all differ. We run them as linked but distinct in every pan-UAE engagement.
