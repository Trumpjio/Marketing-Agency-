Kuwait · City

Hawalli.
Kuwait's densest commercial engine. And its most diverse audience.

Hawalli is the largest city by population in Kuwait at over 1.08 million people. The governorate's 17 districts are the most densely populated in Kuwait, home to much of the country's Arab and Asian expatriate communities, and the commercial centre for retail, F&B, SMEs and technology.

Who they serve

The customers behind the market.

Hawalli's buyers skew more diverse than Kuwait City — Egyptian, Syrian, Iraqi, Lebanese, Filipino, Indian, Pakistani, Nepali and Bangladeshi communities make up a large share. Price-sensitive but brand-aware. Family-focused. Mobile-first and messaging-heavy.
