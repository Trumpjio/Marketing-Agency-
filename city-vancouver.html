The Vancouver market

What makes Vancouver different.

Vancouver's real estate market is one of the most internationally-exposed in North America. Buyer flows from Asia, the Middle East and increasingly from GCC family offices make it a specific marketing environment: tri-language, trust-first, long buying cycles.

OrcaPulse works in Vancouver where the international-buyer angle is real: luxury real estate marketed to Middle Eastern and Asian buyers, clean tech and renewables with GCC export markets, premium lifestyle brands, and film and media businesses connected to Gulf investment.

Media mix in Vancouver

Channels that work.

Google is central. Instagram and YouTube drive luxury property. WeChat has outsized importance for Mandarin-speaking segments. Arabic-language creative moves measurably for Middle Eastern buyer segments. Property marketing here is a tri-language exercise.

Common questions

About marketing in Vancouver.

Why hire a GCC-native agency in Vancouver?+

For Middle Eastern and Gulf buyer segments in luxury property, generalist Vancouver agencies rely on translated English brochures. That underperforms. Native-language, native-cultural marketing to Gulf buyers moves meaningfully more of the right traffic.

Can you work alongside existing Mandarin-language marketing?+

Yes. Most Vancouver luxury property programmes already have Mandarin partners. We add the Arabic and Middle Eastern cultural layer that is typically missing.

Do you work with film and production businesses?+

Yes, where there is GCC investment or GCC-market distribution. Vancouver's film industry has growing Gulf connections and the marketing to Gulf investors or audiences is specific.

How is Vancouver different from Toronto for marketing?+

More international-buyer-driven. Longer buying cycles. Softer overall media market. Less diaspora-density than Toronto for Middle Eastern communities but more international-HNW flow. Media mix emphasises YouTube and Instagram more heavily.

What budgets fit Vancouver work?+

Depends heavily on segment and property price point. Luxury real estate campaigns can be modest in media spend but creative-production heavy. On a call we will walk you through a realistic range.
