The Australia market

What makes Australia different.

Australia and the GCC trade more every year. Direct flights have multiplied. GCC property buyers are increasingly active in Sydney and Melbourne. Australian brands in fintech, F&B, education, mining services and lifestyle are selling into the Gulf. The corridor is real.

OrcaPulse works in Australia where the GCC connection matters: Australian brands selling into the Gulf, GCC property buyers in Sydney and Melbourne, Australian universities marketing to GCC students, and Middle Eastern diaspora consumer markets (especially Melbourne).

Media landscape

Channels and platforms in Australia.

Google is dense. Instagram and TikTok dominate consumer. LinkedIn is strong for professional services. YouTube Shorts is growing fast. Australian consumers are among the most sophisticated in digital-first categories.

Common questions

About marketing in Australia.

Why would an Australian brand hire a GCC agency?+

If a meaningful chunk of your addressable market is in the Gulf — GCC students, GCC property buyers, GCC export customers — a generalist Australian agency will market them the same way they market Sydney shoppers. That fails. We don't.

Do you understand Australian consumer culture?+

We bring in local Australian producers for creative, UGC and influencer work. The strategy, media architecture and cross-market coordination sit with us. Hybrid teams work better than pretending we understand Bondi the way a local does.

Can you handle university-sector GCC marketing?+

Yes. International student recruitment from the GCC into Australian universities is a specific category we work in. It needs Arabic creative, local parent-focused messaging, and credible on-the-ground presence — not just English prospectuses.

How is Melbourne different from Sydney?+

Melbourne is more culturally diverse, has a much stronger Middle Eastern diaspora, is Australia's design and F&B capital, and skews younger digital-native. Sydney is the financial capital, more international, and leads in professional services. The media mix should differ.

What is the budget expectation for Australian work?+

Lower than US, often comparable to major GCC cities. Depends heavily on category. On a call we will give you a realistic range.
