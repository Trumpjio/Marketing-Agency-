Looking for an Arabic SEO playbook?

Arabic SEO is a quiet moat for GCC marketers who do it well. Here is what works in 2026, and what the typical translated-English programme gets wrong.

Book a strategy call
All insights

Translated English SEO fails Arabic rankings.

The most common Arabic SEO mistake is translating an English content programme into Arabic and expecting equivalent rankings. It does not work. Arabic search intent is different. Arabic query structure is different. Arabic competitors are different. A translated page competes against pages written for the audience from the start.

What Arabic search actually looks like.

Mixed-language queries are common (Arabic noun + English brand name). Dialect matters — Saudi Arabic, Gulf Arabic and Egyptian Arabic are not the same for search intent. Voice-input is a rising share, and voice queries skew more colloquial. Google's Arabic index is less saturated than its English index in many commercial categories — which is the opportunity.

The content that ranks in Arabic.

Original Arabic writing produced by native-language writers who understand the audience, not translated English. Longer-form content that answers the full query (Arabic searchers often read deeply). Culturally-specific context — Ramadan timing for retail, Hajj logistics for tourism, local regulation for finance. Faster site speed matters more because mobile network conditions vary more.

The technical layer most agencies skip.

Proper right-to-left HTML. Arabic font loading optimised. Schema markup in Arabic where appropriate. hreflang tags set correctly for Arabic vs. English pages. Localised metadata — Arabic title tags, Arabic descriptions, Arabic image alt text. Each one of these is a routine miss.

The compounding advantage of doing it right.

Arabic search traffic in commercial GCC categories is less competed-for than English. A programme that produces excellent native Arabic content ranks faster and holds longer than the English equivalent would in the same category. For brands serving GCC audiences, this is one of the quietest high-ROI moats available.
