The USA market

What makes United States different.

The United States is the biggest, most expensive and most competitive media market in the world. CPMs are the highest. Attention is the hardest-won. The gap between a sharp campaign and an average one is paid for in millions of dollars.

We don't try to be a general US agency. There are already excellent ones. OrcaPulse works in two places where our GCC-native perspective is a genuine edge: with GCC family offices and institutions investing in US assets (property, hospitality, financial services), and with US brands that want to sell into the Gulf.

Common questions

About marketing in United States.

Why would a GCC agency work in the US?+

Most of our US work is for GCC-connected clients: family offices with US property or operating assets, US brands marketing into the Gulf, or hospitality groups with meaningful GCC guest demand. That's the perspective we add. We don't compete with general US agencies in their core market.

Do you have a presence in the US?+

We work from the GCC and partner with specialist US producers for in-market execution when needed. Most of our client decision-makers are either in the GCC or at GCC-connected US entities, so we meet them where they already are.

How is the US different from the GCC for marketing?+

CPMs are 2-5x higher, attention is harder to win, and the regulatory and privacy environment is tighter (CCPA, state-by-state rules). First-party data strategy is non-optional. The gap between a sophisticated campaign and an average one is also wider — so paid work has to be excellent, not adequate.

What budgets does US work typically require?+

Budgets scale with market, category and goal. US CPMs are among the highest in the world, so a working media programme needs real scale. We will walk you through a realistic range for your situation on a 30-minute call.

Can you handle multi-market campaigns across US and GCC?+

Yes — this is actually where we are strongest. A single team coordinating a US launch tied back to GCC HQ, or a GCC asset marketed to US buyers, avoids the usual translation losses between two separate agencies.
