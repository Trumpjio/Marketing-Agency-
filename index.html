Questions

Common questions.

What does a growth marketing agency actually do?+

We plan and run the marketing that brings you customers: search and AI discovery, paid media, your website, content, social and brand. The difference from a traditional agency is that we are measured on revenue and bookings, not on likes or clicks.

Who will work on my account?+

A senior person plans and runs the work. The partner you speak to before you sign is the partner who runs your engagement. You are not handed to a junior account manager.

How does your pricing work?+

We charge one flat fee per quarter. We do not take a percentage of your ad spend and we do not bill by the hour. The fee depends on which services and markets are in scope. We give you a clear number after the first call.

How do you measure results?+

Against the numbers that matter to your business: qualified leads, direct bookings, closed deals, cost to win a customer and gross margin. We agree the targets with you in the first 90 days and report on them every quarter.

How long until we see results?+

Paid campaigns can show early signals within weeks. Search, content and brand work usually need a few months to build. We tell you which results to expect first and which take longer, so there are no surprises.

Which industries and countries do you work in?+

We work with ambitious businesses across several industries, including real estate, hospitality, dental and aesthetic clinics and fintech. We are rooted in the GCC and also work across the US, Canada, Australia and New Zealand. If your industry is not listed, ask us. We will tell you honestly if we are the right fit.

What happens on the first call?+

It is a 30-minute conversation. We ask about your business and where your growth is leaking. We give you our honest view and tell you whether we can help. There is no sales pitch.

Ready to grow?

We take on 3 new partnerships per quarter. Thirty minutes, no pitch: a frank conversation about where your growth is leaking and what it would take to seal it.
