FAQ

Questions, answered.

What platforms do you build on?+
WordPress with ACF Pro for marketing sites and content-heavy builds. Shopify or Shopify + headless for commerce. Framer or Webflow when the scope is right. We do not use Elementor, Divi or marketplace themes — they destroy performance.
How long does a build take?+
A focused marketing site is 6–10 weeks end to end. A full commerce or SaaS platform is 10–16 weeks. We give you a scoped timeline before you commit.
Do you handle ongoing maintenance?+
Yes — small retainer that covers security updates, uptime monitoring, backups, performance tuning and small CRO improvements each month.
What about SEO on the new site?+
Baked in from day one — schema, Core Web Vitals, semantic HTML, redirect mapping, llms.txt. If you also want us running the SEO program, that is handled by the Search & Discovery team.
