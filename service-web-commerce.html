FAQ

What platforms do you build on?+
WordPress with ACF Pro for marketing sites. Shopify or headless for commerce. Framer or Webflow when scope fits. Never Elementor, Divi, or marketplace themes.
How long does a build take?+
A focused marketing site: 6–10 weeks. Full commerce or SaaS: 10–16 weeks.
Do you handle ongoing maintenance?+
Yes — small retainer covering security, uptime, backups, performance, small CRO.
What about SEO on the new site?+
Baked in from day one — schema, Core Web Vitals, semantic HTML, redirect mapping.
