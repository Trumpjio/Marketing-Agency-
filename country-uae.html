The market in plain words

How United Arab Emirates actually works.

The UAE is where the Gulf's digital marketing money gets spent first. Dubai leads on retail, real estate, hospitality and e-commerce. Abu Dhabi anchors government, energy, finance and culture. Together they attract most of the regional spend and most of the regional competition.

Buyers here are affluent, mobile-first and multilingual. English is the business language, Arabic is the market language, and in many categories you need both. Research now starts in Google, Instagram, YouTube and, increasingly, in AI tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity.

What this means for business owners: generic campaigns waste money fast here. CPMs are among the highest in the region. You cannot outspend the market — you have to out-think it.

Customer behaviour

How buyers actually make decisions here.

If your marketing ignores these, it won't work in United Arab Emirates. Simple as that.

01 —

Mobile-first, always.

Nearly every buyer journey starts on a phone. Desktop is for consideration and checkout, not discovery.

02 —

Multilingual.

A UAE campaign might need to work for English, Arabic, Hindi, Russian and Mandarin audiences — with real cultural nuance, not translation.

03 —

AI-ready buyers.

Perplexity, ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews are everyday research tools for Dubai's affluent segment. If you are not showing up there, you are invisible to them.

04 —

Research-heavy.

Big-ticket decisions (property, cars, healthcare, finance) involve weeks of research across many platforms before a human conversation.

The right approach

How to actually market in United Arab Emirates.

What works, what wastes money, and what we actually do differently.

01 —

Lead with brand.

Pure performance marketing burns cash in the UAE. Buyers choose brands they recognise. Build the brand first, then run performance on top.

02 —

Pay for creative, not just media.

The agencies winning here spend as much on production as on paid. Thumb-stopping creative beats thumb-stopping budgets.

03 —

Build for AI search day one.

FAQ pages, schema markup, llms.txt and clear positioning. Agencies that still ignore AEO are losing visibility they will struggle to earn back.

04 —

Bilingual by default.

English-only brands cap themselves. Arabic-only brands cap themselves. The winners publish both, properly.
