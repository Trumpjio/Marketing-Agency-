How we structure the first ninety days of every engagement.

A field guide. The audits we run, the questions we ask, and the first deliverable that decides the next twelve months.

Dec 2025 11 min read By OrcaPulse
Essay
90

Every OrcaPulse engagement starts the same way. Not because we're inflexible because the first ninety days determine the next twelve months, and we've learned the hard way that skipping steps costs everyone later.

Days 1–14: Diagnose

Three audits, run in parallel.

The brand audit

Positioning, narrative, voice, visual identity, website, content. We look at how the brand actually appears in-market not how the brand book says it should appear.

The acquisition audit

Every paid account. Every pixel. Every conversion event. Server-side tracking. Attribution model. Spend by channel. Return by channel. We find the leaks.

The buyer audit

Interviews with 10–20 of the client's best customers. What they searched. What made them decide. What they almost didn't buy because of.

We produce one document at the end of this phase: a diagnostic. Honest, specific, uncomfortable where it has to be. No fluff, no 'opportunities for improvement' hedging.

Days 15–45: Position

The hardest and most important phase. We sharpen the thing worth saying.

This usually means killing two or three positioning claims that have accumulated over the years and don't work any more. Clients find this uncomfortable; everyone finds it necessary.

Deliverables at the end of this phase:

  • A one-page positioning statement the single sentence we build everything else around.
  • A messaging architecture the three to five supporting claims that back it up.
  • Audience segmentation who we are and are not for.
  • A content pillar framework the topics we own, and why.
Most engagements that fail at month twelve failed at week six because the positioning was never sharpened. We refuse to skip this.

Days 46–75: Build

The engine. Depending on scope, this might include:

  • Website updates or rebuild.
  • Schema and AEO/GEO infrastructure.
  • Rebuilt paid accounts with new audience architecture.
  • Initial content and SEO pillar pages.
  • First wave of creative production static, motion, UGC briefs.
  • Reporting dashboards wired to revenue, not just vanity metrics.

By day 75, everything should be in place to run.

Days 76–90: Launch and read

The first 15 days of actual market exposure. We run everything, we don't over-optimise, and we read the data honestly.

At the end of day 90, we produce a second document: the ninety-day read. What's working, what isn't, what we're changing in month four. This document decides whether we keep going as planned or re-cut.

What this doesn't look like

It doesn't look like a Gantt chart with a thousand tasks. It doesn't look like a daily status call. It doesn't look like a different person running each phase.

It looks like a senior team that stays on the engagement from week one to year three. That's the model.

What this costs

A ninety-day diagnostic, position and build engagement runs around $40–120K depending on scope and complexity. This is a flat fee, not an hourly estimate. It covers everything in the three phases above.

Clients who complete it typically stay with us for 18+ months on a quarterly retainer. The ninety days is where that commitment gets earned.

If you're hiring an agency

Ask them how they structure the first ninety days. If the answer is 'we jump straight into execution' walk. The engagements that fail are the ones that skipped diagnosis.
