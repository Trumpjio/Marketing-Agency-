The Wellington market

What makes Wellington different.

Wellington is small, dense, and the home of a surprisingly successful B2B SaaS scene (Xero was born here). It is also the capital, meaning government, policy, legal and professional services dominate. The city punches far above its weight in B2B exports.

OrcaPulse works in Wellington where the GCC-connected opportunity is clear: NZ B2B SaaS marketing into the Gulf, government and professional-services firms with GCC business, film and creative businesses with Gulf investment, and Wellington's rapidly-growing fintech cluster.

Media mix in Wellington

Channels that work.

LinkedIn is central for the B2B SaaS and professional-services ecosystem. Google is dominant for research-heavy categories. Podcasts and long-form content carry outsized weight in the Wellington market. Content-led demand works here better than almost anywhere else in NZ.

Common questions

About marketing in Wellington.

Why hire a GCC-native agency in Wellington?+

Wellington B2B SaaS and professional services firms with GCC growth ambitions routinely underperform in the Gulf because the ANZ demand-generation playbook does not translate. We bridge that.

Can you handle NZ B2B SaaS marketing into the GCC?+

Yes. This is a specific category. GCC enterprise buyers evaluate differently from ANZ buyers — longer cycles, more trust-dependent, PR-heavy, conference-heavy. Positioning, content, LinkedIn and account-based all look different.

Do you work with professional services firms?+

Yes, where the GCC angle is live. NZ law firms, accounting firms and consultancies with Gulf clients need marketing that reads credible to Gulf principals — different from how they market in Wellington itself.

How is Wellington different from Auckland for marketing?+

Much more B2B-weighted. Smaller consumer market. Government and professional services dominate. LinkedIn and podcast-led demand carry more weight. Content investment pays off faster here than almost anywhere in NZ.

What are Wellington campaign budgets like?+

Depends almost entirely on category. B2B SaaS campaigns into the GCC can be moderate in media but heavy in content and conference investment. We will walk through a realistic shape on a call.
