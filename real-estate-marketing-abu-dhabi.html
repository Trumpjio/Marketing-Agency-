Our minimum paid media spend is $25K per month. Full-service engagements including SEO, paid, web and content start at $40K per quarter. The specific mix depends on your current state and what we need to build first.

Do you work only in Abu Dhabi or across the GCC?+

We work across the full GCC. Abu Dhabi is often the starting point, and most real estate brands we work with expand into neighbouring markets within the first year. Our team is set up to scale with that geography.
