What AEO actually looks like in practice.

A working teardown of how one B2B brand in Riyadh went from invisible to cited across Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT Search.

Feb 2026 9 min read By OrcaPulse
Essay
{ }

Six months ago, our client a Riyadh-based B2B fintech was invisible to AI search engines. Zero citations in Perplexity. Zero visibility in Google AI Overviews. Zero presence in ChatGPT Search answers for any of their category queries.

Today they're cited by name in all three for the top-of-funnel queries that matter. Here's exactly what we did.

What AEO and GEO actually mean

AEO Answer Engine Optimization. Ranking inside tools like Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT Search when a buyer asks a direct question.

GEO Generative Engine Optimization. Being the source LLMs cite when generating answers across the web.

These are not the same as SEO. The signals are related but different. The content format is different. And in the GCC specifically especially in Doha and Riyadh the share of buyer research happening in AI tools has already outgrown what most agencies realise.

The six moves that worked

1. We mapped the questions our buyer actually asks

Not keywords. Questions. Full-sentence, natural-language questions. 'What is the best open banking API in Saudi Arabia for a mid-sized retailer?' not 'open banking API KSA'.

We used three sources: client interviews with their 20 best customers, Reddit and X conversations, and the 'People Also Ask' and 'Related questions' blocks across the AI search tools themselves.

2. We built one page per question

Each page is 1,500–3,000 words, structured as a direct answer up top, followed by the full context. Direct answer first because that's the format AI engines extract.

3. We added rigorous schema

FAQPage schema, Article schema with named author (with a real bio page), Organization schema, Breadcrumbs. All validated. All deployed consistently.

4. We published an llms.txt

At the root of the domain. Listing the key pages, author bios, and the entities we wanted LLM crawlers to understand. Still an emerging standard; now a competitive edge.

5. We built the author's authority

Named author with a real bio, LinkedIn, published work elsewhere. LLM citations correlate with author authority signals even when the user doesn't see the author.

6. We measured relentlessly

Monthly checks across the top 30 target queries in Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT Search. Not just rankings presence, citation phrasing, and whether competitors were being quoted instead of us.

If your schema and FAQ pages aren't right, you are invisible to AI search. Even if you rank #1 in Google.

What we got wrong early

Three things.

First, we wrote content that was too general. 'What is open banking?' is a question. 'What is open banking in Saudi Arabia under Vision 2030?' is a better question for an AEO page targeting our client's actual buyer.

Second, we underweighted structured data. Pretty content without proper schema doesn't earn AI citations. The signal matters as much as the words.

Third, we were slow to add llms.txt. Early movers won't own this forever but owning it in early 2026 was a meaningful advantage.

What this unlocks

Beyond citations, AEO investment compounds into regular SEO. The structured, question-first content ranks well in Google proper. The schema improves rich snippets. The authority signals help the whole domain.

If you're starting from zero

  • Map 20 real questions your buyers ask. Not 20 keywords.
  • Build one page per question, direct answer up top.
  • FAQPage schema on every page. Validated.
  • Named author with a bio page.
  • Publish an llms.txt.
  • Measure monthly across Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search.

Six months. Done well, you'll start showing up.
