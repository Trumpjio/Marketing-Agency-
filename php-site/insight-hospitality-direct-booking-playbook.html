Looking for a hospitality marketing playbook for 2026?

How premium hotels and tourism operators grow direct-booking share — and what the OTA-dependent competitors keep getting wrong.

Book a strategy call
All insights

The real competitor is Booking.com, not another hotel.

Most hospitality operators think of competition as the hotel next door. The honest competitor is the OTA that is converting your branded traffic at a 15-20% commission. If a guest Googles your hotel and clicks a Booking.com result, you have lost margin on that reservation forever.

Three levers that move direct-booking share.

Branded search dominance (own your hotel name on Google). Metasearch presence (Google Hotel Ads, Trivago, Kayak). A direct-booking engine that converts at least as well as the OTA checkout. Each lever is a project; together they recover meaningful margin.

The creative that converts hotel bookings.

Hero photography and video that look premium at thumbnail scale. Property films that show the real property, not stock lifestyle. Review-forward creative — including showing scores. Price transparency where it works for your category. Specific room-type creative for high-value suites and seasonal packages.

The content layer most hotels skip.

Local-area content — neighbourhood guides, nearby experiences, insider recommendations. This content captures intent at the dreaming and planning stages and funnels toward direct bookings. OTAs do not produce it; hotels that do take permanent share.

The metric that reveals real health.

Direct-booking share of total room revenue. If it is dropping, you are losing margin whatever the top-line growth looks like. If it is growing while total revenue grows, the brand is compounding. Everything else is noise.
