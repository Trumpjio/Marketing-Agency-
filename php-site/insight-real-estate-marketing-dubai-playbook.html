Looking for a real estate marketing playbook for Dubai?

A practical playbook for developers and brokers in Dubai — channels, buyer segments, neighbourhoods, and the mistakes most campaigns still make.

Book a strategy call
All insights

The Dubai property buyer is not one person.

A Palm Jumeirah buyer is not the same as a Dubai Hills buyer is not the same as a JLT buyer. Lumping them into a single "Dubai real estate" campaign is why most developer paid-media spend underperforms. The honest first step is dividing the audience by neighbourhood and buyer archetype.

The channels that compound.

Google Search for high-intent branded and neighbourhood searches. Instagram for lifestyle framing. YouTube for property walk-throughs. LinkedIn for off-plan ultra-premium buyers. Snapchat for local Emirati family audiences. The exact mix depends on the project. The common mistake is spreading evenly across all of them.

The channels that waste money.

Generic Google display across Dubai. Facebook feed ads with no video. TikTok for products older-aged buyers never see. Programmatic buys without specific CRM retargeting. These are symptoms of a plan built by media buyers instead of by marketers.

What top developers are doing that most are not.

Dedicated project-specific landing pages. Named relationship managers paired to WhatsApp. Arabic creative for Gulf buyers alongside English for international buyers. Retargeting by funnel stage — not just by site visit. CRM-connected measurement, not platform-reported leads.

The one metric to watch.

Qualified site visits booked — not clicks, not leads. Clicks lie, leads lie, but a prospect who shows up at a sales gallery has signalled something real. If your paid programme is not raising this number quarter over quarter, something upstream is broken.
