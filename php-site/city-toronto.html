Common questions

About marketing in Toronto.

What makes Toronto different for marketing?+

Toronto has a Middle Eastern and Muslim diaspora large enough to be a market in itself, and generalist agencies routinely treat it as 'ethnic marketing' as an afterthought. For Shariah-compliant finance, halal brands and diaspora-targeted consumer, that is a huge miss.

Can you handle Shariah-compliant marketing?+

Yes. Shariah compliance is a credibility and product-level test, not a creative overlay. We work with scholarly sign-off where needed and build creative that reads credibly to Muslim audiences, not generic brand work with a halal badge.

Do you work in Arabic for Toronto audiences?+

Yes. For diaspora targeting, Arabic creative and SEO work materially outperforms English-only, particularly in family-oriented decisions (school, bank, home). We bring in native Arabic copy and production.

How is Toronto different from New York for marketing?+

Less expensive media. More personal. Diaspora is more concentrated and more reachable. LinkedIn density is lower; Facebook and WhatsApp density is higher. Patience over spend is rewarded more.

Can you also market a Toronto brand into the GCC?+

Yes — this is a core use case for us. Canadian clean tech, fintech, education and professional-services brands often have real GCC demand and no infrastructure to serve it. We bridge that.
