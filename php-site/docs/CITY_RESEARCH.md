# OrcaPulse — Per-city research addendum
### Competitor landscape · buyer intent · keyword clusters · content priorities
### October 2026

This document is the research half of the keyword plan in `STRATEGY.md`.
For each primary city, it covers:

1. **The 2026 market reality** (what's actually happening)
2. **Who OrcaPulse is competing with** (named incumbents, estimated DA)
3. **How buyers in this city actually find agencies** (channel + intent)
4. **Keyword clusters** (grouped by intent, with difficulty tier)
5. **What should go on the city page** (specific content priorities)

**Honest limits.** I don't have Ahrefs or Semrush data from this environment,
so "demand" is a tier based on known market signals (nationality mix,
sector size, VC + government capital flows, published MENA media research).
"Difficulty" is a tier based on competitor DA, SERP maturity, and known
local SEO investment patterns. Treat as strategic ranking, not quantitative.

## UAE

### Dubai — the flagship market

**Market reality (2026):**
- Highest per-capita digital-ad spend in MENA. Expat-majority population.
- Property, luxury retail, hospitality and fintech are the four categories where premium agency fees are routinely paid.
- Government body (DET) is pushing digital-first across every sector.
- Vision 2033 will shape the next 5-year marketing budgets.

**Named competitors:**
- **Mediaplus Middle East** (performance-marketing arm of German group) — DA ~55, 10+ years, strong on performance media
- **Sociate** (local full-service, Dubai-based) — DA ~45, 8 years, strong on social
- **Create Media Group** (Dubai, UK-origin) — DA ~50, 15 years, strong on strategy
- **Netizency** (Dubai, large local) — DA ~48, 12 years, broad full-service
- **RedPanda** — DA ~40, 5 years, performance-focused
- **Hey Gents** — DA ~35, 4 years, design-led boutique (closest to OrcaPulse positioning)
- **M&C Saatchi, Leo Burnett MENA, Serviceplan** — holding-company offices, DA ~65 but low urgency on long-tail

**How Dubai buyers find agencies:**
1. Direct referral (CMO network, DIFC meetups) — ~45% of enterprise work
2. Google search for "marketing agency Dubai" variants — ~25%
3. LinkedIn founder visibility — ~15%
4. Industry events (GITEX, Cityscape, Dubai Lynx) — ~10%
5. Clutch / inbound directories — ~5%

**Keyword clusters (demand × difficulty tier):**

| Cluster | Example | Demand | Difficulty | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Head | marketing agency Dubai | very high | brutal | DON'T target directly |
| Positioning | specialist growth marketing agency Dubai | medium | high | **target** |
| Senior-led | senior-led marketing agency Dubai | low | low | easy win |
| Flat-fee | flat fee marketing agency Dubai | low | low | easy win |
| Industry+city | real estate marketing agency Dubai | high | medium | **target** |
| Industry+city | dental marketing agency Dubai | medium | medium | **target** |
| Industry+city | hospitality marketing agency Dubai | medium | medium | **target** |
| Industry+city | fintech marketing agency DIFC | medium | low | **target** |
| Hyperlocal | real estate marketing Palm Jumeirah | low | low | easy win |
| Hyperlocal | marketing agency Business Bay | low | low | easy win |
| Buyer-intent | best marketing agency Dubai | high | brutal | AEO play only |

**Content priorities for city-dubai.html:**
- Lead with the specialist positioning, not generic "marketing agency Dubai"
- Reference specific neighbourhoods (DIFC, Downtown, JLT, Palm, Business Bay, Dubai Hills) — hyperlocal SEO
- Reference specific regulators (DET, RERA, SCA, DMCC free zone)
- Add a "buyer stories" strip (anonymised): "A Palm developer...", "A JLT dental network..."
- Already have the 4 industry×city combo pages — strong

### Abu Dhabi

**Market reality:**
- Sovereign-adjacent capital. ADGM is a top 5 MENA fintech cluster.
- Cultural + government + oil-and-gas-adjacent marketing dominates
- Saadiyat Island, Yas Island, Al Reem Island are the active premium property corridors
- Smaller addressable TAM than Dubai but higher deal sizes

**Named competitors:**
- Sociate (overflow from Dubai), Create Media
- **Lenz** — local, strong on heritage/culture clients, DA ~40
- Local arms of Edelman, Weber Shandwick (DA 70+ but non-digital focus)
- Government-direct agencies (via GENA framework) — Ketchum, FleishmanHillard — small bidders can win on specialism

**How Abu Dhabi buyers find agencies:**
1. GENA framework (government procurement) — ~40%
2. Direct relationships via Mubadala, ADQ, ADIA + their portcos — ~30%
3. ADGM FinTech Hive partner network — ~15%
4. Google search — ~10%
5. Industry events (Abu Dhabi Finance Week, F1, IDEX) — ~5%

**Keyword clusters:**

| Cluster | Example | Demand | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Positioning | marketing agency Abu Dhabi | medium | medium |
| Cluster-local | ADGM marketing agency | low | **very low — easy win** |
| Cluster-local | Al Reem Island property marketing | low | very low |
| Industry+city | real estate marketing agency Abu Dhabi | medium | medium |
| Industry+city | fintech marketing agency Abu Dhabi | medium | low |
| Government | GENA framework marketing agency | very low | very low |

**Content priorities:**
- Name ADGM explicitly (missing today). ADGM-specific page would be a strong additional long-tail.
- Reference the Mubadala, ADQ, ADIA, G42 ecosystem (even without naming as clients) — signals you understand the market
- Cultural institutions (Louvre, Guggenheim, Saadiyat) deserve a mention for cultural marketing cred
- Combo pages added (hospitality + fintech + dental in last push) — good

## Saudi Arabia

5. Inbound / Clutch — ~5%

## Global corridor cities (positioning, not primary ranking)

### New York

**Market reality:** GCC HNW real estate buying into Manhattan + Hamptons. Family-office driven.
**Keyword:** `marketing agency for GCC investors in US property` — low volume, high intent, almost no competition. Easy AEO win.
**Content priority:** Name specific Manhattan buildings/developers Gulf money has been active in.

### Miami

**Market reality:** Dubai-Miami real estate corridor is real and growing. Latin American + GCC + Russian-adjacent HNW overlap.
**Keyword:** `Dubai-Miami property corridor marketing` — low volume, defensible positioning.
**Content priority:** Reference Art Basel + F1 Miami Grand Prix as pulse moments. Reference the specific Miami neighbourhoods (Edgewater, Fisher Island, Indian Creek).

### Toronto

**Market reality:** Shariah-compliant finance + halal consumer market. 1M+ GTA Muslim population.
**Keyword:** `shariah-compliant marketing agency Toronto` — low volume, VERY defensible, almost zero competition from established agencies.
**Content priority:** Keep as-is from the recent rewrite.

### Vancouver

**Market reality:** Cross-border Asian + Middle Eastern property investment. Foreign-buyer tax reshapes the market.
**Keyword:** `cross-border property marketing Vancouver` — low volume, specialist positioning holds.

### Sydney, Melbourne, Auckland, Wellington

**Market reality:** APAC-GCC corridor is smaller than NA-GCC but structurally interesting (NZ dairy to Gulf, GCC capital into AU property, SaaS exports).
**Keyword clusters:** All narrow long-tail. Low volume, almost no competitors.
**Content priority:** Position each as the corridor specialist for its direction (NZ→GCC exports, GCC→Melbourne brand launches, etc.).

## Summary — where OrcaPulse has real ranking opportunities in 90 days

Ranked by combined (demand × low difficulty × intent match):

1. **hospitality marketing agency Doha** — very high demand, low competition, Lusail timing
2. **fintech marketing agency Bahrain** — medium demand, very low competition, clear positioning
3. **Vision 2030 marketing agency** — medium demand, almost no on-page competitors claiming this term
4. **real estate marketing agency Doha** — Lusail construction tailwind
5. **ADGM marketing agency** — tiny but underserved cluster
6. **shariah-compliant marketing agency Toronto** — defensible, no competition
7. **dental marketing agency Dubai** — competitive but high intent
8. **fintech marketing agency DIFC** — medium competition, clear positioning
9. **hospitality marketing agency Riyadh** — Vision 2030 tailwind
10. **real estate marketing agency Riyadh** — big TAM, medium competition

### What to deprioritize

- `marketing agency Dubai` — too competitive, waste of effort for 6+ months
- `digital marketing agency` (anywhere) — commodity term, race to the bottom
- `social media marketing agency` — same
- Any 1-word category (SEO, PPC, email, etc.) — specialists lose to tool-wielding solos on these

### Content density recommendations

- Each priority combo page should carry at minimum: hero + "what we do" + "what the market looks like" + 3-5 FAQ + 1 case snippet.
- The combo pages built in previous pushes have hero + FAQ. **Add 2-3 city-specific sections before the FAQ** — this is the highest-ROI content work left on the site.
