Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information. Last updated: January 2026.

Who we are

OrcaPulse (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a growth marketing agency. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and share information about you when you visit our website or engage us for services.

Information we collect

We collect three types of information:

  • Information you give us. When you book a strategy call, enquire about our services, or email us, we collect the name, email, company, phone number and context you share.
  • Information collected automatically. When you visit the site, we collect standard analytics data: IP address, browser type, pages viewed, time on site, referring source. We use this to understand how the site is used, not to identify you personally.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. We use essential cookies for the site to function and analytics cookies to measure traffic. You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings.

How we use information

  • To respond to your enquiry and schedule a call.
  • To deliver services if we enter into an engagement.
  • To send occasional updates about our work (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To improve our website and content, in aggregate form.
  • To meet legal and tax obligations.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Who we share information with

We share information only with:

  • Service providers that help us run the business (hosting, email, analytics, scheduling). These providers are bound by contract to protect your information.
  • Legal and regulatory authorities where required by law.

Data retention

We keep enquiry information for up to twelve months after your last contact with us. Client engagement data is kept for the duration of the engagement plus seven years for tax and legal purposes, then securely deleted.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct any inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal retention obligations).
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at help@orcapulse.ai.

International transfers

We operate across the GCC and beyond. Where personal information is transferred outside your country of residence, we rely on standard contractual clauses and appropriate safeguards to protect it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach that affects you, we will notify you promptly and in line with applicable law.

Children

Our site and services are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top will reflect the most recent revision. Material changes will be flagged on the site.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about how we handle your data, go to help@orcapulse.ai.
