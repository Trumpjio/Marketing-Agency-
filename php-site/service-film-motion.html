Who it's for & when it works

Honest about fit.

Not everyone should buy this. Here's who it works for and when we'd tell you to spend the money differently.

Right fit when

  • You have a brand that benefits from premium production.
  • You can commit to a monthly or quarterly production cadence.
  • You understand that social-first and TV-first are different crafts.
  • You're willing to invest in editorial choices, not just technical execution.
  • You need multiple format variants, not one hero spot.

Wrong fit when

  • You want a TV spot reformatted for social.
  • You expect premium quality on a smartphone budget.
  • You have no clear creative direction and want us to guess.
  • You need the film shipped by Friday.
  • You can't review and approve within 48 hours per round.

Getting started

The first 30 days.

Three things happen when we start: we identify the tests, gather the data, and set expectations against a realistic timeline.

What we test
  • Hook test which 3-second opener holds attention.
  • Format test talking head vs product-led vs documentary.
  • Length test 15s vs 30s vs 60s performance.
  • Caption test captions-on vs captions-off engagement.
  • Platform-native edit vs reformatted edit performance.
What we need from you
  • Brand guidelines, logos, type, color.
  • Any existing brand films, product footage, b-roll library.
  • Target audience profile and platforms.
  • Campaign objective (awareness, consideration, conversion).
  • Approval workflow and timeline.

What goes wrong

The common mistakes.

The patterns we see repeated across underperforming programs. Fix any of these and performance usually moves immediately.

01

Reformatting a TV spot for social.

Different grammar. Different pacing. Different hooks. It never works.

02

Burning the budget on one hero asset.

Modern campaigns need 10+ variants per concept. One beautiful film ≠ a campaign.

03

Not shooting for the edit.

Days are expensive. Shoot a shot list the edit needs, not everything that looks nice.

04

Ignoring sound design.

Vertical social video with weak audio bombs. Sound is half the craft.

Questions, answered

Common questions.

The real questions prospects ask us before signing an engagement.

What size projects do you take?+

From a single-location founder film to a multi-city campaign. We'll tell you honestly if a scope is too big for us; most agencies won't.

Do you handle paid-media creative?+

Yes social-first creative is a core offering. Shot and edited for platform, not reformatted from a TV spot.

What about motion graphics only?+

Yes. Explainers, product animations, title sequences, logo work.

Who owns the final work?+

You do, in full. We license music, buy out talent and hand over the master files.

Can you shoot in multiple GCC markets?+

Yes. We have crew networks across UAE, KSA, Qatar and Bahrain. We can also plan for compliant shoots where regulations differ by market.

How much does a campaign cost?+

Full-stack campaigns (concept + shoot + 10+ format variants) typically start at $60K for scoped programs. Simpler shoots start lower.

Can you work with our existing agency's concept?+

Yes, but we'll be honest if the concept has issues before we execute it. Shooting a bad brief doesn't help you.

Ready to grow?

We take on a limited number of partnerships each quarter. Thirty minutes, no pitch a frank conversation about where your growth is leaking and what it would take to seal it.
