Lusail.
A new city, built for the next generation of buyers.

Lusail is a purpose-built premium city with distinct real estate, hospitality and lifestyle dynamics separate from Doha proper. Over 38 square kilometres, 19 districts, and a planned population of around 450,000 — including 170,000 professionals and 80,000 visitors to entertainment and retail.

Who they serve

The customers behind the market.

Lusail skews younger, more premium, and more mobile-first than central Doha. Young Qatari professionals buying their first premium home. Expatriate families attracted to the planned-city lifestyle. Visitors drawn to Entertainment City, Marina, and the retail boulevard.
