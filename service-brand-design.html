FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a brand project take?+
8–14 weeks for most engagements, depending on scope and research depth. Rush timelines are possible but produce worse work.
Do you do rebrands or new brands only?+
Both. For rebrands, we look hard at what equity exists before touching it — most brands lose more by changing too much than by changing too little.
What about brand strategy without design?+
Yes — strategy-only engagements are possible. We write the positioning, narrative and personality document, and your existing design team or another agency executes.
What size organizations do you work with?+
From founder-led startups preparing to raise, to mid-market businesses rebranding at $20–200M revenue.
