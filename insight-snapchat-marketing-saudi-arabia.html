Looking for a Snapchat marketing playbook for Saudi Arabia?

Snapchat is still the under-indexed platform in Saudi marketing plans. Here is what Saudi brands get right — and what agencies abroad consistently miss.

Book a strategy call
All insights

Snapchat in Saudi is not Snapchat in the West.

Western marketers mentally slot Snapchat as a declining teen platform. In Saudi Arabia it is one of the dominant social platforms across all age brackets, used daily by a large share of the adult population. For local reach, it rivals or beats Instagram in many categories. Treating it as an afterthought leaves meaningful volume on the table.

What performs on Saudi Snapchat.

Vertical video that feels native to the platform — not Instagram creative repurposed. Local Arabic voice. Short cycles — new creative weekly, not quarterly. Partnership with Saudi Snapchat creators who understand their audience. Shoppable integrations where the catalogue supports it.

The categories that win.

F&B launches. Fashion drops. Beauty and personal care. Automotive. Local retail promotions. Entertainment and events. For B2C with urgency and local Saudi targeting, Snapchat is often the first channel we scale.

The categories that struggle.

Enterprise B2B. Luxury financial services. Any category where buyer research is long and considered. Snapchat is a fast-decision channel. Trying to run a Saudi B2B SaaS programme on it is a misread.

The implementation gap most agencies have.

They run Snapchat as a budget sub-line of Meta campaigns. They repurpose Facebook creative. They do not have Arabic-speaking creative producers. They do not have local Saudi creator partnerships. These are the common failures, and they are what separates a $20K/month Snapchat programme that works from one that doesn't.
