The Canada market

What makes Canada different.

Canada is quieter than the US, less saturated and often underrated for premium marketing. It is also home to one of the largest Middle Eastern diasporas outside the region, significant GCC investment flows (particularly real estate), and a growing Shariah-compliant finance sector.

OrcaPulse works in Canada where the GCC angle matters: for Islamic finance and Shariah-compliant brands, for GCC family offices with Canadian property, for Canadian brands marketing into the Gulf, and for Middle Eastern diaspora-serving consumer brands.

Media landscape

Channels and platforms in Canada.

Google is the default. LinkedIn works well for B2B. Facebook is still bigger in Canada than most people assume. Multi-language work is routine — English, French and Arabic for diaspora segments.

Common questions

About marketing in Canada.

What makes a GCC-native agency relevant in Canada?+

Three things: a large Middle Eastern diaspora that marketers often misread, strong Islamic finance momentum where the cultural fluency matters, and heavy GCC-origin property investment that is marketed badly by generalist agencies. Those are the places where we add something nobody else does.

Do you work in French as well as English?+

For Quebec-facing work, yes — we bring in native French producers. Most of our Canadian work is English-first with Arabic-language work for diaspora segments.

Can you help a Canadian brand enter the GCC?+

Yes. This is one of the things we are built for. Canadian brands in clean tech, fintech, education and luxury consistently underperform in the Gulf because they market the way they market at home. We bridge that.

How do you handle Islamic finance marketing specifically?+

Shariah compliance is a credibility test, not a tagline. We work with scholars for product-level sign-off where relevant and build creative that reads credibly to Muslim audiences — not generic brand work with a halal badge dropped on top.

What budgets do we need for Canadian campaigns?+

Smaller than US work, but still real. The exact number depends on category, geography and goal. We will give you a specific range on a 30-minute call.
