Looking to launch a GCC brand in Melbourne?

Melbourne is the natural launch city for GCC F&B, retail and lifestyle brands entering Australia. Here is what works — and what consistently fails.

About marketing in Melbourne · All insights

Why Melbourne first, not Sydney.

For GCC-origin F&B, retail, fashion and lifestyle brands, Melbourne is almost always the better launch city than Sydney. Three reasons: a much larger Middle Eastern diaspora, a more culturally diverse and open consumer base, and a design-and-food-led city culture that rewards premium and story-led brands.

Diaspora is not your whole market, but it is your foundation.

The Lebanese-Australian and broader Middle Eastern diaspora in Melbourne is big enough to validate a launch. But the strategic mistake is treating it as the ceiling. The real play is: use the diaspora as the foundation, then expand into the broader Melbourne consumer through strong brand storytelling, product quality and design-led creative.

The creative that works.

Instagram-first. Short-form video. Food brands succeed with sensory, confident, authentic creative. Fashion brands succeed with design-led, premium production. Retail brands succeed when they lean into their origin story rather than erasing it. Pan-Australian cookie-cutter creative underperforms in Melbourne.

The channels that work.

Instagram and TikTok dominate consumer. Facebook has outsized reach in diaspora communities. In-market partnerships with Melbourne-based Middle Eastern diaspora creators move meaningful volume. Press and PR in Melbourne-based design and food publications carry weight.

The common failures.

Marketing the brand the way it markets in Dubai. Over-indexing on diaspora without the second-wave expansion plan. Under-investing in product localisation (menu, sizes, format). Launching with heavy paid before the organic and PR layer is in place. These are all fixable, but routine.
