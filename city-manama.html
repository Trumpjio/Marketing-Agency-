Manama.
Where fintech talks to the rest of the Gulf.

Manama is Bahrain's commercial and financial capital, and the heart of the GCC's most dynamic fintech ecosystem. Single-regulator agility (CBB), fast approvals, and an innovation-focused posture have made it the preferred launch pad for payments, open banking, wealth and regtech plays targeting the wider region.
