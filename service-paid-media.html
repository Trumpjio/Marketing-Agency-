FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is the minimum media spend you work with?+
As a rule, $25K/month in paid media or more — below that, our fee eats too much of the budget to be worth it for you.
Which platforms do you specialize in?+
Google (Search, Shopping, Performance Max, YouTube), Meta (Facebook/Instagram), LinkedIn, TikTok, and major programmatic DSPs. We run them as one portfolio, not individual channel plays.
How do you handle attribution?+
Server-side tracking (GA4, server events, Conversions API), plus a weighted model that reconciles platform-reported conversions with your real revenue data. We report the real number, not the inflated one.
Do you produce the creative?+
Yes. Static, motion, UGC and iterations. Creative is where most paid programs win or lose; outsourcing it elsewhere kills the feedback loop.
