What goes wrong

The common mistakes.

The patterns we see repeated across underperforming programs. Fix any of these and performance usually moves immediately.

01

Chasing keywords, not entities.

Modern search is entity-driven. Keyword lists miss how AI engines read the web.

02

Publishing AI-generated content.

Google and the LLMs down-rank it. Buyers notice.

03

Ignoring author authority.

AI engines cite named humans with credentials. Anonymous content loses.

04

No schema, or broken schema.

Rich results, FAQ snippets and AI citations all depend on structured data.

Questions, answered

Common questions.

The real questions prospects ask us before signing an engagement.

What's the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?+

SEO is Google and Bing rankings. AEO is Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Google AI Overviews showing up when buyers ask direct questions. GEO is being the source LLMs cite when they generate answers. Same infrastructure, three surfaces.

Do you guarantee rankings?+

No reputable agency does. We guarantee the method, the pace and the transparency not an outcome Google controls.

How long until I see results?+

Technical and schema wins land in weeks. Rankings on competitive terms over 3–6 months. Compounding gains past month 9.

Can you fix our existing content?+

Often faster than writing new. A content audit usually finds 20–40% of existing pages that can be rewritten for immediate ranking gains.

What about Arabic SEO?+

Yes. We work with native Arabic writers for both MSA and dialect (Najdi, Hejazi, Khaleeji) depending on target market. Arabic AEO is still open ground in most GCC categories.

Will AI replace SEO?+

AI is reshaping it, not killing it. The underlying skill entities, schema, authority, quality content matters more than ever, not less.

Do you buy backlinks?+

No. We earn them through content and digital PR. Buying links is a short-term win and a long-term liability.
