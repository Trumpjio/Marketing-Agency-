FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is AEO and GEO, and why do they matter now?+
AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is ranking inside tools like Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT Search. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is being the source LLMs cite when generating answers. Combined, they already account for a meaningful share of buyer research — and that share is growing every quarter.
How long until we see results?+
Technical and schema wins land in weeks. Rankings for competitive terms typically move over 3–6 months, with compounding gains past month 9. We report monthly on leading indicators so you can see the engine working before the revenue follows.
Do you guarantee rankings?+
No reputable agency does. We guarantee the method, the pace, and the transparency — not an outcome Google controls.
How is this priced?+
A flat quarterly retainer scoped to the size and competitiveness of your market. No hourly billing, no per-link pricing, no bait-and-switch upsells.
